# Operating guide

## First run

1. In **Characters**, save character names and passwords. Uncheck **Can craft bars** for unregistered characters. They still launch and contribute gold or existing bars, but only registered crafters buy bars. An unregistered character can also be the courier. Password files are encrypted for your Windows user; SQLite contains no passwords.
2. In **Settings**, select the same supported DATester 7.41 `Darkages.exe` used by DAQuester. The included server profile and routes are for that client. Set the courier, banker mode, and correct weight per bar. For Local mode, save an enabled banker in Characters; DAMiner handles travel to Abel bank and selects Lamont automatically. For External mode, enter the already-running banker's name, map ID, and approximate coordinates.
3. In **Record setup**, choose a saved character and click **Launch and record**. The manager launches it and signs it in. A configured local banker with a saved password is launched too, so you can accept the sample trade in its window.
4. In that game window, go to Abel bank, open the bar-making NPC, and manually create one bar. Then complete a sample bar trade with your banker, accepting it in that window or through its existing automation; the recorder verifies server acceptance and the exact inventory change.
5. Return to the manager. Review the learned NPC, map, menu actions, gold cost, bar name, and verified sample batch. Click **Save learned setup**.
6. Press **Go**. Enabled characters launch and sign in concurrently; only native login input is serialized to protect desktop focus. DAMiner reads every login location and protects the courier's travel funds before concurrent crafting begins. Characters starting in **Medenia** use **Kelan at Noam Bank (map 10061)**, near the free interior floor at **8,8**. Other characters use Lamont at Abel Storage, preferring a free reachable tile on **row 8** near **6,8**. Gold is pooled within each bank group for further craft rounds. The courier visits the producers to collect their bars, then delivers to your configured banker, including an external banker in Temuair.
7. Only the courier trades to your banker. **Local** mode walks the banker to Abel bank, parks on an available row-8 tile, and waits for Lamont. The courier approaches its observed floor position; DAMiner verifies both sides of the trade and deposits the Gold Bars through Lamont. It confirms every deposit before another batch. **External** mode sends bars to the configured map/coordinates and waits the chosen interval after each accepted batch; the banker's own automation handles acceptance and deposits.

New installations choose Local mode and require your own banker name. Existing profiles keep their saved external behavior. For a banker already automated on another computer or VPS, choose **External** and enter its character name, map, coordinates, batch size, and deposit wait. No external banker password or VPS connection is needed on the sending computer.

Local bankers never craft or donate gold. DAMiner deposits existing carried Gold Bars as well as incoming batches; other items are left alone. The local banker uses Abel's safe floor destination near **6,8**, away from the exits and Lamont's counter. Deposits require Lamont to be visible on **map 167** and use the same slot-based merchant protocol as DAOrganizer, with server-confirmed inventory changes and about 1.1 seconds between requests. Unsupported or ambiguous responses stop the run and preserve the ledger for review. Validate this on your server before unattended use.

Medenia selection follows recorded local routes, including caravans, rather than map numbers. Each character keeps its login bank while the courier travels. Noam uses the saved purchase actions, prices, and exact dialog-text guards with Kelan's identity; a changed menu stops before submitting that step. Leftovers from different bank groups remain carried rather than making producers cross continents to pool them.

For mixed regions or a courier starting in Medenia, DAMiner reserves **100,000 gold** on the courier before crafting. It can fund this reserve from characters using the same bank. If that group cannot supply it, fund the courier before Go. This reserve is excluded from crafting and pooling; actual travel still checks each fresh quoted fare and available gold. Ship, carpet, and caravan travel use observed NPC menus and confirm the actual arrival. Expected connection handoffs resume on the same character's fresh connection without repeating payment. Only exact quoted fares with unchanged inventory count toward travel spending in the final totals. Submitted passage is journaled before sending; uncertain passage remains reviewable after reopening the app.

At a travel stop such as Quard's Express Ship, the walker follows the validated interior approach before resolving an NPC that has not appeared correctly. If necessary, it walks closer to the recorded NPC anchor on free floor, using acknowledged steps and stopping when fresh NPC evidence is usable. It avoids exit triggers, occupied tiles, and the anchor itself. Missing or unreachable NPCs stop this bounded approach with map and coordinate details; no unseen NPC is clicked and no fare is submitted during discovery.

Travel NPC visibility matches DAQuester's ten-tile screen range. The three-tile walking approach radius is not an interaction requirement: Quard stands behind a counter and can be opened from visible public floor. The NPC must still be uniquely observed on the current map; menu identity, fare, and arrival checks remain required.

The **Use** checkboxes on the **Operations** home screen enable or disable saved characters for the next run. Changes save automatically and appear on the Characters page too. Disabled characters are skipped; their accounts stay in the roster. These controls lock while a run or recording is active. Keep the chosen courier enabled, or select another enabled courier in Characters.

**Fast mode** defaults on for each character, matching DAQuester. Its checkbox saves automatically and can change while moving or recording. It uses fast walking in clear areas, slows near other players, and keeps normal pace in restricted areas. Your own currently connected DAMiner characters do not cause the nearby-player slowdown. A saved but disconnected character does. The pace description shows the current condition. Crafting, deposits, and trade verification keep their existing server evidence requirements.

Recording uses direct game protocol observation; no MCP installation is required. Login and chat packets are excluded. A verified sample establishes a usable batch, not the server's absolute maximum. Record a larger successful trade to increase it. Dialog replay requires the recorded NPC and exact dialog text; changed menus stop the run and require new recording.

Verified craft progress saves automatically as a draft. **Stop recording** preserves it; **Resume recording** keeps the craft and lets you finish the trade later. Once both steps are verified, **Save learned setup** works even after Stop or reopening the app. The status beside the controls explains any missing step. Incomplete drafts do not enable Go.

**Pause** finishes the current atomic operation before pausing. **Stop** cancels commands and waits, leaving game clients open. An uncertain operation is preserved in History; inspect affected characters before acknowledging it and requesting a fresh run. Existing clients from other apps cannot be attached.

Unregistered donors hand off enough gold to fund each purchase without exceeding the receiver's carrying cap. This handoff remains active when **Pool leftover gold** is off; that setting controls pooling between registered crafters. Insufficient gold remains carried. If no registered crafter is enabled and enough gold exists for a bar, the run stops before moving it.

The server's explicit **Please register your character to use this feature** refusal switches that character to donor behavior. A refused craft is recorded as **Rejected** only when a fresh read confirms unchanged gold and bar counts. Timeouts and changed balances still require review. Missing login expiration text alone does not establish registration status; use the saved checkbox for known unregistered characters.

After each verified craft, the manager finishes informational **Next** pages and closes the final bank confirmation. It never chooses another purchase during cleanup. The ledger retains the verified craft if popup cleanup fails, and the run stops before further spending.

Local trades are verified from exact fresh changes on both managed characters, even when the server omits acceptance echoes. External banker trades still require server acceptance/completion and the courier's exact change. **You have exchanged** and **Exchange completed.** are recognized as completion responses in exchange packets and server system messages. Their popups are dismissed after the operation is Verified. Delayed popups remain eligible for cleanup before the next trade; an unrelated popup does not discard the verified trade's cleanup evidence. Popup text alone never proves a transfer. Unrelated or stale popups are left alone.

An interrupted trade's History entry identifies the stage and whether acceptance may have reached the server. A user Stop remains a cancellation. Ambiguous outcomes still require inspection; DAMiner never resends them automatically.

Before accepting a local gold trade, the receiver's server-confirmed exact one-way offer can establish the amount when the sender's echo is missing. Extra items, reverse gold, conflicting amounts, and cancelled offers still stop the trade. Errors identify the failed setup or verification stage.

Trade startup waits for both managed players to see each other. If the server silently ignores an empty opening request, DAMiner can send up to three opening requests, two seconds apart, for two managed characters. Recovery ends as soon as either side receives any exchange evidence. Both identities, gold balances, complete inventories, visibility, and closed-window state must still match immediately before a repeated request is sent. External banker trades use one opening request. Gold offers, item offers, and acceptance are never replayed by this recovery.

After managed collection movement, startup also waits for both characters' own server positions and both player lists to show trade range. A courier's movement acknowledgement may precede its updated position in the producer's view. This settling wait is bounded by five seconds or the configured trade timeout, whichever is shorter. Changed identities or connections stop immediately. The two-tile range remains strict once opening begins.

Response stages use the configured trade timeout, normally 120 seconds, with a ten-minute upper bound. A fresh explicit server refusal is reported directly. If a window does not open, the error identifies which managed window responded and includes the most recent allowed server notice, even if it did not contain the words trade or exchange. An existing open exchange stops a new request. Acknowledging History permits your next run; it does not repair a server refusal or authorize replay of an ambiguous transfer.

Player approach stops on a free tile within two tiles of the visible player's current position. The walker reselects its destination if that player moves or another character occupies the planned tile. It does not continue walking closer once trade range is reached.

Per-character locks let independent movement and NPC dialogs overlap. A local trade locks both participants in a consistent order. One failed producer cancels and awaits the others before the run returns. Collection and banker delivery stay coordinated through the single courier. DAQuester's observed world-map nodes and acknowledged walker handle travel; the bank's floor destination is separate from Lamont's NPC position.

Navigation now includes current DAQuester routes and validated Excalibur legacy exit hints. A hint with an unknown landing is followed one map at a time, with a new plan from the actual server-confirmed position. Unexpected destinations stop travel. The packaged `content/navigation-coverage.json` accounts for all 1,531 referenced map IDs, including records without dimensions or collision data. Its 731 bank connections describe walking topology, excluding paid NPC services; blocked terrain, server restrictions, and incomplete metadata can still prevent travel. Unsupported areas need verified route/geometry information before DAMiner can walk from them.

When a destination allows nearby standing tiles, world-map selection plans to that map first. It resolves the permitted floor tile from the actual arrival, current occupancy and exit triggers. A blocked approximate target therefore cannot reject a valid world-map destination or redirect the courier through other towns. Navigation that requires an exact tile retains that requirement.
