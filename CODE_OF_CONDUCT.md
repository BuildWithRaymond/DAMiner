# Community code of conduct

DAMiner welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements, and give feedback about the work rather than the person. Help newcomers understand the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing private information, and personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not post another player's credentials, account data, private messages, or identifying captures.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban participants according to the conduct and impact. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so will not reveal private information.

Report concerns privately to [BuildWithRaymond](https://github.com/BuildWithRaymond) through a publicly listed contact method. If none is available, request a private contact channel without describing the incident publicly.
